Group Project Team Formation

How the activity works

Maximize learner agency while minimizing public rejection and deadlocks.

Cohort cards Read playbook
Before

Build the Cohort Webpage

Every learner contributes a profile card through Git/GitHub. Team Leader candidates add extra answers about facilitation.

13:00

Browse Cards and Elect Leaders

The class reviews cards for working style, interests, goals, and leader answers. Leader voting stays anonymous.

13:40

Submit Private Preferences

Non-leaders rank their top three Team Leaders while using cards for context, not public popularity.

13:55

Form Teams Through Structured Invites

Teams invite privately using preferences plus card information. Learners choose privately if they receive multiple invitations.

Round 1: leader invites Round 2: pair agrees Round 3: team majority
15:20

Complete Remaining Matches

Unassigned learners rank remaining teams, teams rank interested learners, and matching continues until everyone has a team.

After break

First Team Meeting

Teams align on availability, roles, communication, Git workflow, decision-making, conflict resolution, and first project ideas.

L Learners

  • Customize one profile card through code.
  • Declare Team Leader interest on the card if volunteering.
  • Browse cards before private votes and preferences.
  • Accept or decline invitations privately.
  • Join team decisions once accepted.

I Instructors

  • Provide the card template and GitHub workflow.
  • Review card PRs for completeness and professionalism.
  • Open and close each phase on time.
  • Monitor team sizes and incomplete matches.
  • Stay neutral: learners choose teams.
Visibility rule

Cards replace public pitches: everyone is seen without live audition pressure.

Decision rule

Consensus is preferred; majority vote or random draw resolves deadlocks.

Privacy rule

Votes, preferences, invitations, accepts, and declines stay private.

Session outcome: 100% assigned, zero manual instructor picks.
Cards inform decisions; Team Leaders organize discussion and are not the boss.
Designed for a single facilitated session
